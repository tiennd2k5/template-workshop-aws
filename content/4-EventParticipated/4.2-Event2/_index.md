### Agenda & Key Presentation Topics:

During the event, participating hackathon teams presented their projects, development journeys, and key experiences.

1. **One Team**
   - **Business Problem:** Customers may abandon the ordering process when they have to leave the chat platform and use a separate channel to place an order.
   - **Solution:** Developed the KFC Bot Agent, an AI-powered conversational ordering agent that allows customers to understand menu requests and place orders directly through messaging platforms.
   - **Key Takeaway:** Learned how AI Agents can go beyond answering questions by understanding user intent, using tools, and executing real business actions through an AWS-based architecture.
2. **3KA Team – S.H.E.P.H.E.R.D**
   - **Business Problem:** Manual monitoring of crowds and queues at large events can be slow, difficult to scale, and prone to missing abnormal situations.
   - **Solution:** Built an AI-powered crowd monitoring system using computer vision for people tracking, crowd density and queue analysis, combined with Amazon SageMaker, Bedrock AgentCore, and Strands Agent for congestion prediction, proactive alerts, and operator assistance.
   - **Key Takeaway:** Learned how Computer Vision and Agentic AI can work together to turn real-time camera data into actionable insights and support faster operational decision-making.
3. **Plan V Team**
   - **Business Problem:** Solution Architects spend too much time manually analyzing requirements, designing architectures, creating diagrams, and estimating AWS costs under tight deadlines.
   - **Solution:** Built an AI-powered assistant that analyzes requirements, generates AWS architectures, Draw.io diagrams, IaC drafts, and cost estimates through a conversational interface.
   - **Key Takeaway:** Learned how Agentic AI and AWS services can automate the architecture design process, reducing manual effort and enabling faster solution delivery.
4. **Signal Scout Team**
   - **Business Problem:** Teams need to detect strategic changes from scattered market, financial, and competitive signals, but manual analysis is time-consuming.
   - **Solution:** Built an AI multi-agent platform that collects evidence, detects signals, and turns scattered data into actionable insights.
   - **Key Takeaway:** Learned how Agentic AI and AWS can automate intelligence analysis while improving scalability, traceability, and cost efficiency.
5. **Six Pillars Team**
   - **Business Problem:** AML investigations are time-consuming because analysts manually review alerts, while most are false positives.
   - **Solution:** Built an AI-powered AML workflow engine that automates investigation, evidence collection, and report generation using a multi-agent architecture with human-in-the-loop validation.
   - **Key Takeaway:** Learned how Agentic AI and AWS can streamline compliance workflows while improving efficiency, explainability, and keeping humans in control.
